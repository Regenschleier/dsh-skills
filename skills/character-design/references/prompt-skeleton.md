# 提示词骨架（八段，中英对照模板）

> 用法：先抄「渲染锚」，再按顺序填八段；每段先写英文，后面跟中文。方括号里是要你替换的部分。

## 渲染锚 / Render anchor

A still from a Japanese 3D animated feature film, a CG-rendered character with semi-realistic materials, never a photograph of a real person and never flat two-dimensional anime artwork.
按日本 3D 动画电影的一帧来渲染：半写实材质的 CG 角色，绝不是真人照片，也绝不是平涂的二维动画。

## 1 人物 / Character

[年龄与骨相] + [发] + [眼] + [皮肤] + [唇] + [耳饰]

A woman of about twenty-two, an adult rather than a child, with a slim neck and a defined jaw that comes to a soft point; near-white pale blonde hair gathered into a loose low bun, fine strands escaping across her forehead; pale grey-green eyes, one clean specular highlight; fair translucent skin with a faint reddish scattering of freckles high on the cheeks and a soft pink blush on the cheekbones; full coral lips slightly parted; one small pearl stud in the ear on the clean side.

约二十二岁的女性、是成年人不是孩子，脖颈细、下颌线清晰并收到一个柔和的尖；近白浅金的头发在脑后松松挽成一个低髻，细发丝从额前逃出来；灰绿的眼睛、一点干净的高光；白皙透亮的皮肤，两颊高处有淡淡的红棕雀斑，颧骨上一层柔和的粉色；唇是饱满的珊瑚色、微微张开；干净那一侧耳朵上只一颗小珍珠耳钉。

## 2 服装 / Wardrobe

[主件] + [内搭] + [材质] + [细节]

She wears an oversized ivory painter smock of heavy washed linen over a cream cotton top, soft and creased, a few pale paint smudges on the near wrist.

她穿一件偏大的象牙白画袍，厚重的洗水亚麻，里面是奶白棉衫，布面柔软、带褶；近侧手腕上有几点浅色颜料印。

## 3 景别与动作 / Framing and action

[机位] + [人物朝向] + [手在做什么] + [桌上有什么]

Medium close-up from behind her near shoulder; her head is turned about forty degrees away from the lens and tilted down toward the paper, the far eye hidden behind the bridge of her nose, only the near eye showing from under a fall of loose hair; her left forearm lies flat on the bright white sheet and her right hand holds a thin brush, drawing a long wet stroke.

中近景，从她近侧肩后拍；头离镜头转开约四十度、低向纸面，远侧眼被鼻梁挡住，只有近侧那只眼睛从垂落的碎发下露出来；左小臂平放在亮白的纸上，右手捏一支细笔，正在画一道长而湿的笔触。

## 4 光 / Light

[方向] + [硬度] + [逆光边缘] + [杂光]

Hard low sun rakes in through the window directly behind her; her hair and the near shoulder burn out to white; a few round green-gold flare orbs drift across the frame; the air between the lens and her is thick with warm haze.

低角度的硬太阳从她正后方的窗户斜射进来；头发和近侧肩膀烧成白色；几颗圆形的绿金光斑飘过画面；镜头与她之间的空气浓着暖雾。

## 5 配色 / Palette

[三个主色] + [一点饱和] + [反差]

Ivory white, pale gold, deep garden green, with a few small saturated dabs of paint on the palette; the frame itself low in contrast.

象牙白、浅金、深庭院绿，调色盘上有几点小而饱和的颜料；画面本身低反差。

## 6 镜头与景深 / Lens and depth

[焦距] + [光圈] + [前景] + [竖幅]

An 85mm lens at maximum aperture; the foreground table, paper, palette and near sleeve completely out of focus, drifting across the frame as large soft bokeh shapes. Vertical 9:16.

85mm 镜头全开光圈；前景的桌面、纸、调色盘和近侧衣袖完全失焦，作为大而柔的散景块飘过画面。竖幅 9:16。

## 7 构图气质 / Mood

[情绪词] + [留白] + [质感]

Quiet, cool, and patient; the frame waits rather than poses; filmic tonemapping, gentle highlight rolloff, faint chromatic fringing.

安静、偏冷、有耐心；画面在等待而不是摆拍；电影化的色调映射，高光柔和滚落，极淡的色散。

## 8 禁止项 / Exclusions

No text, no watermark, no logo, no extra fingers, no second character, no jewellery other than the one pearl stud.

不要文字、不要水印、不要 logo、不要多余的手指、不要第二个人、除那颗珍珠耳钉外不要别的首饰。

## 变体：暗调莫兰迪（不要再写瓷面）

把渲染锚换成：

Shot through a dusty matte filter and printed two stops under; the world is a dark grey wash and only her skin and the gloss of her lips stay luminous and warm; the brightest tone in the frame is no brighter than pale smoke.
透过一层蒙尘的哑光滤镜拍摄，并且压暗两档印制；世界是一片暗灰，只有她的皮肤和唇上的光泽保持明亮而温润；画面里最亮的调子也不亮过淡烟色。

同时删掉 `porcelain / no pores / glowy`，否则「暗」和「瓷」会在同一张图上打架，出来就是恐怖谷。
